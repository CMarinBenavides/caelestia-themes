Pledge 1 · All murder, war and robbery are forbidden in this world.
Pledge 2 · All conflicts will be settled by victory and defeat in games.
Pledge 3 · In games, each side will wager something it agrees is of equal value.
Pledge 4 · As long as it does not violate the third pledge, anything may be wagered and any game may be played.
Pledge 5 · The challenged party has the right to decide the game.
Pledge 6 · Wagers made in accordance with the pledges must be upheld.
Pledge 7 · Conflicts between groups will be settled by representatives with absolute authority.
Pledge 8 · Being caught cheating during a game means defeat.
Pledge 9 · In the name of God, these rules may never be changed.
Pledge 10 · Let's all have fun playing together!
